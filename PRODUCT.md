# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Guests in Germany looking for a place to eat: families and groups wanting a grill dinner, couples at breakfast, friends meeting for coffee and dessert. They arrive mostly on a phone, often deciding between two or three restaurants. They want to feel the room before they walk in, find the dish and the price fast, and reserve without friction.

## Product Purpose
The public website for RÜYA Cafe & Restaurant, a modern Turkish-European kitchen. It carries the same identity as the printed menu and turns it into a site that sells the room: the warmth, the grill, the coffee culture, the dessert craft. Success = a guest browses the full menu, trusts the place, and reserves or calls.

## Brand Personality
Warm, generous, quietly luxurious. Turkish hospitality ("Misafir bereketle gelir") meets European restraint. Voice is host-like and specific, never salesy. Three words: warm, crafted, generous.

## Anti-references
- The dark "smoke & grill steakhouse" cliche (charcoal black + neon gold). The old site leaned this way; the brand's own printed menu is the opposite: light, warm, marble.
- Generic AI restaurant landing: flat beige body, Playfair/Cormorant italic, tiny uppercase eyebrows over every section, identical card grids.
- Editorial-magazine affectation (broadsheet grid, mono metadata labels). This is a restaurant, not a journal.

## Design Principles
1. **The printed menu is the brand.** Match the physical piece: cream marble, gold rule frame, warm brown serifs, numbered bilingual dishes. The site should feel like the menu come to life.
2. **Material over decoration.** Warmth comes from a real marble surface, gold leaf accents, and food photography, not from tinting everything beige.
3. **Bilingual by design.** German and Turkish sit side by side everywhere, as they do on the table.
4. **The menu is the centre.** 142 dishes, fast to search and filter, easy to read on a phone. Everything else points here or to reservation.
5. **Hospitality, not conversion funnels.** Reserve and call are always one tap away; the tone invites rather than pushes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA: body text ≥4.5:1 against marble (warm brown ink on cream, verified). Full keyboard nav, visible focus rings in gold. `prefers-reduced-motion` honoured on every animation (smooth scroll, reveals, hero motion degrade to static). Bilingual labelling; `lang` attributes correct. Touch targets ≥44px.
